=====================================================
  PrioTask - Mobile Installation Guide
=====================================================

METHOD 1: Instant PWA Install (Recommended)
1. On your Motorola phone, open Google Chrome.
2. Visit: https://priotask-eight.vercel.app
3. You will see an 'Install App' button right in the header, OR:
   Tap the Chrome menu (three dots ⋮ in the top right).
4. Tap 'Install app' or 'Add to Home screen'.
5. PrioTask will be installed as a full standalone app
   with its own app icon and splash screen!

METHOD 2: Offline Launcher
Open 'PrioTask-Launcher.html' directly in your phone's browser.
